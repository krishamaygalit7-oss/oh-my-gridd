HOW TO PLAY OH MY GRID!

Think it through. Place each number. Make every line 15!

  1. Your goal is to complete a 3×3 Magic Square.
  2. Use the numbers from 1 to 9.
  3. Place each number into one of the nine boxes.
  4. Each number can only be used once.
  5. Every row, column, and diagonal must total exactly 15.
  6. Watch the sum counters to monitor your progress.
  7. Complete all eight lines to win!

ABOUT OH MY GRID!

A little logic. A little math. A whole lot of 15.

OH MY GRID! is an interactive mathematics puzzle game that challenges players to arrange numbers from 1 to 9 in a 3×3 Magic Square. Players must use logical thinking, problem-solving skills, and basic mathematics to make every row, column, and diagonal equal 15.
